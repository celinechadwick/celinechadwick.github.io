Meet our Team

We are committed to fostering a collaborative ecosystem that unites top experts in workforce development. We create dynamic partnerships that address the evolving needs of the workforce with the tools to build lasting financial security—where better health and improved pay work together to support long-term economic and social well-being.

Senior Advisors

Steve Bella

A Principal at Pacific Policy Network, Steve has more than 25 years of experience in communications and public policy, specializing in education, economic development, and healthcare. Known for his cross-sector collaboration, he has advised governors and shaped policy strategies for state agencies, nonprofits, and corporate clients.

Earl Buford

A workforce leader and subject matter expert, Earl currently leads CAEL (Council on Adult Experiential Learning). He has championed economic growth through partnerships with workforce developers, educators, and employers. His leadership as CEO of Workforce Development Boards in Milwaukee and Pittsburgh demonstrates his expertise in systems alignment and multi-sector collaboration. He also serves on several national workforce advisory boards.

Tracey Carey

Tracey leads Midwest Urban Strategies (MUS), a consortium of 13 major urban workforce boards, driving systems change with over $30 million in funding. With deep expertise in workforce strategy and business engagement, she strengthens cross-sector collaboration to expand and diversify talent pipelines.

Michael Holmes

A recognized expert in workforce development and systems alignment, Michael has held executive roles in nonprofits and government, including as CEO of St. Louis Training and Employment Services. His leadership in comprehensive service delivery extends across local, state, and national levels.

Jenna Morey

Jenna brings expertise in workforce development, international economic initiatives, and nonprofit leadership. She has led apprenticeship expansion and served on the Oklahoma Governor’s Workforce Development Council, advancing cross-sector workforce strategies that align industry needs with public systems.

Executive Director

Anne Rascón

Anne specializes in integrating policies, programs, and resources across workforce development, education, and the healthcare system. With experience spanning public, private, and nonprofit sectors, she has led nationwide workforce training initiatives and advised the U.S. Department of Labor, national employers, and workforce boards to develop and implement training and education solutions.
