About Us

Better Health Better Pay (BHBP) is a nonprofit collaborative helping Managed Care Organizations, Medicaid agencies, and workforce leaders unite around a shared mission: improving health and employment outcomes through cross-sector innovation.

We serve as a trusted partner for those navigating the rapidly evolving Medicaid landscape-offering strategic insight, national expertise, and practical tools to drive measurable results.

What We Do

We help organizations design and implement systems that improve health and economic outcomes-especially for Medicaid populations.

  • Align healthcare, workforce, and human services systems
  • Design self-sufficiency frameworks and measurement tools
  • Strengthen the direct care workforce (recruit, train, retain)
  • Support Medicaid demonstrations, waivers, and cross-sector initiatives

Ascend Score™

  • The Ascend Score™ is a proprietary assessment framework offered to health care organizations, government agencies, and community platforms to evaluate and track member self‑sufficiency. It enables case managers and program staff to establish a baseline of individual stability, measure progress over time, and support referrals to community resources. Designed to align with Medicaid managed care and other whole‑person care initiatives, the Ascend Score™ provides actionable insights that support quality improvement, compliance, and equity monitoring.
