Measuring Self-Sufficiency. Tracking Progress. Informing Action.

A standardized assessment and scoring tool used to personalize healthcare and the provision of medical information concerning social determinants of health by evaluating and tracking member self-sufficiency.

Better Health Better Pay Collaborative helps healthcare organizations, government agencies, and community partners establish a baseline of individual self-sufficiency, measure changes over time, and identify opportunities for additional support through the collection, reporting, and analysis of healthcare data related to social determinants of health and economic self-sufficiency.

Our services provide administrative and care coordination support to healthcare organizations, including Medicaid managed care organizations, by helping them understand and interpret member self-sufficiency data and related Medicaid outcomes. Better Health Better Pay Collaborative offers implementation guidance and technical assistance to participating organizations.

Contact us to learn more about licensing health care advisory services for tracking self-sufficiency and related outcomes.

We help organizations improve health and economic outcomes, especially for Medicaid populations.

  • Align healthcare, workforce, and human services systems through the collection, reporting and analysis of healthcare-related survey data.
  • Strengthen the direct care workforce through recruitment, training, and retention initiatives.
  • Support Medicaid demonstrations, quality improvement initiatives, and partnerships with community organizations.
